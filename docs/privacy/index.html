AKASHA

Privacy policy

Last updated November 9, 2026. Applies to Akasha Dictation for iPhone and Akasha for Mac.

Akasha collects no data. Your voice and your words stay on your device. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no third-party SDK that collects anything.

What stays on your device

  • Audio. Speech is transcribed on the device and the audio is discarded. Nothing is recorded to disk unless you turn on "Keep audio" in settings, in which case it is encrypted and deleted after 24 hours.
  • Transcripts and history. Your dictation history is encrypted with a key that never leaves the device, and is deleted after 30 days by default (you can change this).
  • Your dictionary, snippets and settings. Stored on the device only.
  • The keyboard (iPhone). The Akasha keyboard has no internet access. Full Access is used only to pass your dictated text from the Akasha app to the keyboard on the same phone.

The only internet connections

WhereWhenWhat is sent
huggingface.coOnce, when you set Akasha upA request for the speech model files. Each file is checked against a fingerprint before use.
github.com (Mac only)About once a dayThe app's name and version, to check for a signed update.
Your own iCloudOnly if you turn on sync (off by default)Your dictionary and snippets. Never history or audio.

Nothing else. Akasha's Settings ▸ Privacy lists every connection it has made (where, why and when, never what you said). Offline mode, on both Mac and iPhone, blocks them all and pauses iCloud sync while dictation keeps working.

Local only, not the internet

  • Share with a computer (iPhone). Lets a browser on a computer on your own Wi-Fi receive text you dictate on that screen. It runs only while that screen is open, answers only devices on your local network, and only a browser that entered the 6-digit code shown on your phone. It sends only the text from that screen: no audio, no history, and nothing goes to the internet.
  • Music while you dictate (Mac, off by default). Turns the Mac's volume down, or pauses Music and Spotify through macOS's own app control, which asks your permission once per app. It tells those apps nothing but pause and play.
  • Smart cleanup (Mac, optional). If you install Ollama, cleanup talks to it on the Mac itself, never over the internet.

Sharing and selling

Akasha does not share, sell or rent any information, because it never receives any.

Children

Akasha collects no information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Delete the app. That removes everything Akasha stored on the device. If you turned on iCloud sync, turn it off first to remove the synced word lists from your iCloud.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: hello@colinrobinson.com.
