iPhone: getting started

  1. Open Akasha and tap Download. The speech models are about 950 MB, so use Wi-Fi and keep the app open until it finishes.
  2. Add the keyboard: Settings ▸ General ▸ Keyboard ▸ Keyboards ▸ Add New Keyboard ▸ Akasha.
  3. Tap Akasha in that list and turn on Allow Full Access. Typing works without it; dictation needs it to get your words from the app.
  4. In any app, switch to the Akasha keyboard with the globe key and tap the mic.

Common questions

The mic button opens the Akasha app. iPhone keyboards can't use the microphone directly, so the first dictation in a while hops to the app to switch it on. Go back, talk, and tap stop. For the next 10 minutes it's one tap.

Does it work offline? Yes. After the first download, dictation needs no internet at all. Try it in Airplane Mode.

How much space does it need? About 1 GB for the speech models.

Mac: I hold fn and nothing types. System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Accessibility, and make sure Akasha is on. If it is, switch it off and on again.

Mac: fn opens Emoji. System Settings ▸ Keyboard ▸ "Press 🌐 key to" ▸ Do Nothing, or pick another talk key in Akasha's Setup window.
